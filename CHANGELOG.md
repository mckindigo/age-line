# Changelog

## v0.2.1
- Tooltips no longer pop up or stick when you tap or click cards. They show only on a long-press (touch) or a hover (mouse), and a long-press never trains the unit.
- Throwers now face the enemy (their knees bent backward, so they looked like they were walking away). Slingers wind up, release exactly where the rock leaves the sling, and follow through. Every ranged unit's shot now leaves from its weapon tip.

## v0.2.0: "Five Ages"
- Five ages: Stone → Kiln → Banner → Gear → Spark. Each has 4 warriors, 3 turrets, a special, its own base and its own world.
- Evolve with XP from kills, then pick 1 of 3 perks while the battle slows down.
- A special power per age (Rockslide, Kiln Burst, Muster Horn, Steam Vent, Static Surge) with telegraphs you can read.
- 4 turret sockets on a tower behind your base. Swap turrets anytime and get 25% back.
- New HUD: base health at the top, one bottom dock, a floating training queue, and a special button with a cooldown ring.
- The rival AI now reads your army and counters it. Hard and Impossible save up for the right unit.
- Bases crack, tilt and burn as they take damage, and collapse into rubble when destroyed.
- New art: parallax backgrounds, weather, muzzle flashes, debris, and a screen-shake setting (On/Low/Off).
- Long matches end: after 20:00, "Dusk Wane" wears down both bases.
- Hotkeys: 1-4 units, Q W E R turrets, V evolve, Space special, P pause.
